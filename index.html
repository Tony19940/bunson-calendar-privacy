English — Privacy Policy

1. Scope and privacy principles

Bunson Calendar is a Khmer lunar calendar and reminder app. You do not need an account. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, remote push, or crash-reporting SDK. We do not operate an app backend that receives your calendar entries, name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We do not sell personal data. Information you choose to type, such as a name in a reminder or card signature, is processed locally. Optional online tools do make the requests described below; “offline calendar” does not mean that every feature is offline.

2. Information on your device

  • A local SQLite database stores reminders (titles, notes, dates, times, recurrence, advance alerts and lunar anchors), anniversaries (names, dates, notes and alert settings), and precept-day journals (date, observance, number of precepts and notes).
  • Birth date and time entered for a birth card are processed in memory. If you explicitly save the birthday as an anniversary, its date/time and lunar anchor are stored in that local database.
  • Local preferences store language, theme, font and elder-mode choices, calendar and festival display options, notification times and sounds, startup/onboarding choices, a background-image path, tool visibility/order, selected province, currency/metal settings, and the optional share-card signature. Weather and market responses are cached locally; market-history points are retained on a rolling 90-day basis when history is updated. Expired caches may remain until refreshed or app data is cleared.
  • Home-screen widgets receive precomputed calendar dates, festival text, theme settings and update metadata through local shared preferences. Scheduled notification content and timing are stored by Android on the device.
  • Photos you select for a frame are selected through the system picker and cropped locally. Selection/cropping and sharing can create temporary files in app cache. Exported card images contain the content you choose to include (which may include a photo, birthday or signature). They stay in device storage until you choose to share them. Saving creates an image in the “Bunson Calendar” album; saved images can outlive uninstalling the app.
  • The app does not upload these records, preferences, photos or journals. Android backup, device transfer, or a gallery/cloud service you enable may copy device data under that service’s settings. The app does not implement its own cloud sync or control those services.

3. Network requests and external destinations

All app-controlled requests below use HTTPS GET, with no account, API key, record payload, photo, signature or device-location lookup. Network services necessarily receive connection information such as the public IP address, request URL, time and HTTP headers. These services may keep their own logs; we do not promise that their processing is transient.

Contact opens a mail app addressed to lz3959823@gmail.com. Browser, email and Play services then process data under their own policies. Tapping Share passes only your chosen card and caption to the app/recipient you select through the system share sheet; that destination may upload or retain them. The calendar app does not send them to its own server.

4. Permissions

Internet access supports the optional weather/rate tools. Notification permission supports alerts you enable. Exact alarms support user-created reminders, anniversary alerts, lunar festival and precept-day alerts at the chosen time; older Android versions may ask for special alarm access. If exact access is unavailable, scheduling falls back to inexact alarms. Boot access restores scheduled alerts and widget updates after restart. Vibration provides notification feedback. Wake locks support brief scheduled/background work; the meditation timer can keep the screen on. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, broad photo-library or storage permissions. Saving generated images uses Android MediaStore without requesting storage permission; on older devices unsupported saves may fail rather than request broad storage access.

5. Control, retention and deletion

You can edit or delete reminders and anniversaries; deleted records remain in a local recycle state for seven days and are purged on a subsequent maintenance run. You can clear the stored card signature by saving an empty signature. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings to remove its database, preferences and caches. Gallery exports, previously shared copies and OS/cloud backups require separate deletion through the relevant gallery, recipient or backup service. There is no app account or developer-hosted calendar database to delete. For third-party network logs, use that provider’s contact/privacy process; deletion is not guaranteed by this app.

6. Children, changes and contact

The app is a general-audience calendar and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information for the developer. Please do not send children’s information to the contact email. Policy updates will be published at the policy URL with a revised effective date. Questions about this app: lz3959823@gmail.com.

中文 — 隐私政策

1. 适用范围与原则

Bunson 农历（Bunson Calendar）是高棉农历和提醒应用，无需注册账户，不含广告、统计分析、追踪、远程推送或崩溃上报 SDK。我们没有接收日历记录、姓名、邮箱、电话、联系人、照片或广告标识符的应用后端，不出售个人数据。用户自行在提醒或卡片签名中填写的姓名等信息在本机处理。天气、汇率等在线工具会发出下述网络请求；离线日历不代表所有功能都不联网。

2. 本机数据

  • SQLite 本地数据库保存提醒的标题、备注、日期时间、重复规则、提前提醒和农历锚点；纪念日的名称、日期、备注和提醒设置；戒日记录的日期、守戒情况、戒条数量和笔记。
  • 出生卡输入的出生日期、时间先在内存处理；只有主动保存为生日纪念日后，日期时间和农历锚点才写入本地数据库。
  • 本地偏好保存语言、主题、字体、长辈模式、日历节日显示、通知时间音效、启动与引导状态、背景图片路径、工具排序显示、省份、货币金属设置和分享签名。天气与行情响应本地缓存；行情历史在更新时滚动保留 90 天。过期缓存可能保留到刷新或清除应用数据时。
  • 桌面小部件通过本地偏好接收预计算日期、节日文字、主题及更新元数据。通知文字和时间由 Android 在设备上保存。
  • 相框照片经系统选择器选取并在本机裁剪；选图、裁剪、分享可能留下应用缓存临时文件。导出卡片含用户选择的照片、生日或签名，主动分享前保存在设备内。保存到“Bunson Calendar”相册的图片可在卸载后继续保留。
  • 应用不上传这些记录、偏好、照片或戒日笔记。用户启用的 Android 备份、设备迁移或相册云服务可能按其设置复制设备数据；本应用没有自建云同步，也不控制这些服务。

3. 网络请求与外部入口

应用发起的请求全部为 HTTPS GET，不含账户、API 密钥、个人记录、照片、签名或设备定位结果。接收服务会看到公网 IP、请求网址、时间、HTTP 请求头等连接信息，可能保留日志，不能承诺仅作瞬时处理。

4. 权限用途

网络权限用于天气、汇率和金属报价。通知权限用于用户开启的提醒。精确闹钟用于用户自建提醒、纪念日、农历节日与戒日准点提醒；较早 Android 版本可能要求特殊闹钟授权，不可用时降级为非精确调度。开机广播用于重启后恢复通知和小部件刷新。振动用于通知反馈；唤醒锁用于短时后台调度，冥想计时器可保持亮屏。不申请定位、通讯录、麦克风、相机、整库照片或存储权限；生成图片通过 Android MediaStore 保存且不申请存储权限，旧设备不支持的保存可能失败，不会改为申请广泛存储访问。

5. 控制、保留与删除

提醒和纪念日可编辑、删除；删除后在本机回收状态保留七天，在后续维护任务中清理。保存空签名可清除已存签名。可在 Android 设置清除应用存储，删除数据库、偏好及缓存。相册导出图、已分享副本和系统/云备份需通过相册、接收方或备份服务另行删除。应用没有可注销账户或开发者托管的日历数据库；第三方网络日志须通过供应商隐私渠道处理，本应用不保证其删除结果。

6. 儿童、更新与联系

本应用为一般受众日历，不会有意为开发者收集儿童个人信息；请勿向联系邮箱发送儿童信息。政策更新将在隐私网址发布并修改生效日期。应用联系邮箱：lz3959823@gmail.com。
